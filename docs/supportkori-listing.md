## Full description (English)

**Bring Windows-style taskbar previews to your Mac.**

Rest your mouse on any Dock icon and Dock Viewer shows a live thumbnail of every window that app has open — including minimized ones. Click a thumbnail to jump straight to that window.

**What you get**
- 🪟 Window previews on hover, for every app
- 🔴🟡🟢 Close, minimize or zoom windows right from the preview
- ➖ Minimized windows are shown too, one click restores them
- 🌐 Browser tab previews for Chrome, Opera, Safari, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi and Arc — click a tab to open it, close tabs, or show them in one row like the Dock
- 📷 Capture a picture of every tab at once
- 🖱 Click the Dock icon of the app you're using to minimize it — click again to bring it back, just like Windows
- ⚡ Choose how fast previews appear
- 🔄 Updates itself automatically
- 💻 Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 14 Sonoma or newer

**Install in 1 minute**
1. Download and unzip, then move **Dock Viewer** to **Applications**
2. Open it. If macOS says it can't verify the developer: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**
3. Allow **Accessibility** and **Screen Recording** when asked
4. Hover any Dock icon 🎉

Full guide with pictures: https://github.com/shakilnextive-beep/DockViewer#installation
